# Literature revision record 25 October 2026

The CV was treated as a discovery list, not as authority for publication metadata or scientific claims. All added references were checked against a publisher or author institution. Verification depth is recorded below; an abstract-only check is not represented as a full-text review.

## Corrections and scope

- Liu et al. (2005): use the published title “Runup and rundown generated by three-dimensional sliding masses”.

- Haridhi et al. (2023): cite the final NHESS article DOI 10.5194/nhess-23-507-2023, replacing the CV discussion-preprint DOI.

- Lo et al. (2026): cite the 2026 journal volume; the DOI contains 2025 because the paper appeared online in December 2025.

- CFDAgent: cite the final Physics of Fluids publication rather than only its earlier arXiv record.

- Wu et al. (2014a) denotes moving-solid coupling; Wu et al. (2014b) denotes the Shuang-Yuan forensic framework. The suffixes are kept consistent across the three documents.

- RFM is a published capability (Huang et al., 2024); mixed RFM–DBM coupling is the future extension.

- Vuong et al. (2024) already reported LES–VOF–DBM complex-pier simulations. The new Guandu draft states this prior work and defines its contribution through field constraints and site scenarios. Authors must compare the published article and current reports at input/figure level before claiming independent datasets or reusing published figures. The checked abstract alone cannot settle overlap.

- Chu et al. (2025) on bidirectional scour is by Hanzeng Chu and collaborators; it is external literature, not the CV author Chia-Ren Chu.

- Existing S-drive prose was retained. Automatic heading numbering and pics/ image links were normalized for a self-contained revision. Earlier versions remain untouched.
